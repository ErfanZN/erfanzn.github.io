# Erfan Zareian portfolio

## Purpose
English hiring portfolio for a Senior Product Designer working in Discovery at SnappMarket. Visitors should quickly understand his judgment, read substantive case studies, and contact him on LinkedIn. A content/marketing site, not a SaaS application.

## Visual direction
Quiet white surface, black typography, generous but purposeful whitespace. The memorable detail is a decision explorer that demonstrates problem → intervention → outcome with a real inspection-tool example. No scroll hijacking, fake cursors, loading theatrics, fabricated product screenshots, skill percentages or animated outcome counters.

## Tokens and ownership
Runtime token authority: dist/styles.css :root. Paper #ffffff, ink #161616, secondary text #5c5c5c, panel #f5f5f5, line #dedede, strong surface #181818. Original company logo colors are retained. Display: Bricolage Grotesque with system sans fallback. Body: DM Sans with system sans fallback. Utility: system monospace. Body at least 16px, regular labels 14px, secondary metadata 12px minimum. Content maximum 1200px. Small controls use 8px corners; project artwork panels use 18px corners. Spacing scale 4,8,12,16,24,32,48,64,96.

## Interaction
Links remain discoverable without hover. Hover gently translates an arrow or raises contrast. Semantic button-based three-step explorer supports keyboard arrow navigation and touch; default content is present without JavaScript. Experience uses native details/summary. Reduced motion disables smooth scrolling and transforms. Natural page scroll and usable visible scrollbars. Every interactive target has a visible focus ring and enough space.

## Responsive behavior
Desktop hero is text plus an intimate portrait. Cards use a two-column grid; first project is featured at full width. At narrow widths the hero, project cards and career rows stack. On mobile, Work and Contact remain in the header; other sections are reachable by natural scrolling. No modal mobile menu. Text must reflow at 200% without hiding controls.

## Content integrity
Use supplied copy and public case studies. Discovery is the focus at SnappMarket. Hamrah Mechanic APM and Product Designer roles were concurrent. Shab title is Product Designer. Keep metrics scoped; 3× flow-level conversion is not +300%. No invented email, CV download or confidential inspector screens. Schematics are explicitly labeled as explanatory diagrams, not product screenshots. Case study pages link to the full original Medium articles.

## Scope
Home plus three case-study pages, functional contact links, selected project summaries, process explorer, professional history and short personal story. Hosted via Sites. No backend, analytics, forms or account system required.
